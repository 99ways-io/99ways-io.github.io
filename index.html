Most businesses can list many things that might improve. The harder problem is finding the few variables that actually matter. We use research to ask better questions, measurement to establish what is happening, experiments to change one important assumption at a time, and engineering to make the answer real.

Open source

99Trace

Every WooCommerce order in PostHog, exactly once, on the visitor who placed it. A WordPress plugin whose server sends purchases, refunds and cancellations, so payment redirects, in-app browsers and ad blockers no longer lose orders, and posthog-js and the server share one visitor ID.

PHP · GPL-2.0-or-later · WordPress / WooCommerce · GitHub

posthog-page-experiments

Code-first page experiments for PostHog. It applies A/B and multivariate variants to existing pages, checks eligibility before recording an exposure, lets reviewers force a variant through the URL, and falls back safely when a flag is late or misconfigured. PostHog keeps assignment and analysis.

TypeScript · MIT · npm: @99ways/posthog-page-experiments

These are independent community projects. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by PostHog or WooCommerce.
